Today, Lorenzo, it is your birthday.

I have been a father for ten years now, and I wrote these few lines of code thinking about you and Tommaso.  
I use them to remind myself, every day, of the father I would like to be for you.

### 01 — Love

```python
def love(child, unconditionally=True):
    child.loved = bool(unconditionally)

for child in children:
    love(child)
```

Love them both without conditions. Even when it is hard. Especially when it is hard.

---

### 02 — Presence

```python
while child.needs_help():
    parent.stay(close=True)
```

When they need you, stay.  
Even when you are not exactly sure what to do.

---

### 03 — Respect

```python
def respect(other):
    treat(other, as_equal=True)

respect(child)
```

Treat them with respect.  
Children are people, with their own dignity, character, and inner world.

---

### 04 — Apology

```python
if parent.is_wrong():
    parent.say("sorry")
```

When you are wrong, apologize.  
Being their father does not mean always being right.

---

### 05 — Patience

```python
while not child.has_learned():
    parent.repeat(kindly=True)
```

Learning takes time.  
Sometimes much more time than we would like.

---

### 06 — Example

```python
child.learn_from(parent.actions)
```

They listen to your words, but they watch your actions even more.

---

### 07 — Listening

```python
if child.wants_to_talk():
    parent.listen(with_attention=True)
```

Listen to them for real.  
Not only to answer, but to understand.

---

### 08 — Trust

```python
if child.falls():
    parent.help(child)
    child.try_again()
```

Help them up, but also let them try again.  
Trust grows when someone feels capable.

---

### 09 — Time

```python
schedule.make_room(for_=child)
```

Time is one of the clearest ways to say: you matter to me.

---

### 10 — Gratitude

```python
for day in shared_life:
    parent.say("thank you")
```

Because being your father is also one of the greatest gifts of my life.
